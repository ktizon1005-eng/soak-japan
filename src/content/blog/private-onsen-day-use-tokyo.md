**This guide is for you if** you want to try a Japanese hot spring but don't want to bathe naked in front of strangers — or you have a tattoo, or you just want time alone with your partner — and you don't want to book a whole ryokan night to get a private bath. By the end you'll know six places near Tokyo where day visitors can rent one, what it costs, and exactly how to book.

A private bath is called **kashikiri-buro** (貸切風呂) in Japanese. You rent it for a set time slot, and only your group uses it.

Every price and rule below was checked on each facility's official website on **October 9, 2026**. Prices change, so confirm on the linked page before you go.

## The short version

| Place | Area | Price | Time | How to book | Tattoos |

| Hakone Yuryo | Hakone-Yumoto | ¥10,400–15,400 per room | 120 min | Online | OK |

**Our pick for most visitors: Hakone Yuryo.** It's the only one we found that has private open-air bath rooms, online booking, and no tattoo restriction.

## 1. Hakone Yuryo — best overall

Hakone Yuryo's private open-air bath rooms (called **Kaden**) can be used on their own, without using the rest of the facility ([official price page](https://hakoneyuryo.jp/price/)).

- **Price:** for 2 people, ¥10,400 on weekdays and ¥11,400 on weekends, for 120 minutes. Rooms for 4 cost ¥12,400–15,400. You can add 30 minutes from ¥2,600.
- **Booking:** online via TableCheck, from one month ahead until 15:00 the day before. After that, call 0460-85-8411.
- **Tattoos:** Hakone Yuryo lifted its tattoo restriction on April 1, 2025.
- **Access:** free shuttle from Hakone-Yumoto Station, about 3 minutes.

## 2. Yunessun Mori no Yu — online booking, eight private baths

Yunessun's traditional bath area, Mori no Yu, has 8 private baths for day visitors ([official page](https://www.yunessun.com/morinoyu/reserved/)).

- **Price:** ¥5,000–11,000 for a 90–120 minute slot, depending on the time. Check whether Mori no Yu admission is included when you book.
- **Booking:** online only — Yunessun does not take phone bookings. The booking site has an English option.
- **Tattoos:** in Mori no Yu, tattoos are allowed only if two of the facility's paid cover seals cover them completely ([official notice](https://www.yunessun.com/news/detail/?CN=413712)). The page doesn't say whether this also applies inside the private baths, so ask when you book.

## 3. Tenseien — cheapest in Hakone-Yumoto, book on the day

Day visitors can use Tenseien's private bath, but pay admission on top ([official price page](https://www.tenseien.co.jp/price/)).

- **Price:** ¥2,200 for 60 minutes, plus admission.
- **Booking:** at the front desk after you check in. We found no way to book in advance.
- **Tattoos:** not allowed in the facility, including tattoo-style stickers.

## 4. Yu no Sato Okada — cheapest of all

Yu no Sato Okada's family bath costs ¥1,500 for 45 minutes, between 11:00 and 23:00 ([official page](https://www.yunosato-y.jp/spa.html)).

- **Booking:** reservation required. The page doesn't say how; call 0460-85-3955.
- **Tattoos:** not allowed in the facility, including sticker or paint tattoos.
- **Access:** shuttle bus from Hakone-Yumoto Station (times vary by day).

## 5. Yutoro (Yugawara) — two private baths

Yutoro's private baths can be rented without using the main facility; you check in at its "Kaze no Garden" counter (official page: yutoro.co.jp/bath2.html — the site may show a security warning in your browser).

- **Price:** ¥3,850 per person (before tax) for 120 minutes, minimum 2 people, 10:00–20:00.
- **Booking:** at the counter. We found no online booking.
- **Tattoos:** not allowed.
- **Access:** community bus from JR Yugawara Station (about 7 minutes) to Yuyu no Sato, then a 1-minute walk.

## 6. Chikurinan Mizuno (Atami) — a ryokan bath by phone

This Atami ryokan lets day visitors use its private bath, though hotel guests get priority ([official page](https://www.mizunoryokan.co.jp/dayspa/chikurin/)).

- **Price:** ¥3,000 per adult for 60 minutes, starting between 13:00 and 18:00. Cash only.
- **Booking:** phone only (0557-67-2643), from 7 days ahead. You may need to speak Japanese, or ask your hotel to call for you.
- **Tattoos:** the page doesn't say. Ask when you call.
- **Access:** free pickup from JR Ajiro Station.

## What about inside Tokyo?

We couldn't confirm any bath inside central Tokyo that rents a private bath to day visitors. If you're based in Tokyo, Hakone-Yumoto is the easiest trip: Hakone Yuryo, Tenseien and Yu no Sato Okada are all a short shuttle ride from the station.

## Tips

- **Book Hakone Yuryo early.** Online booking opens one month ahead and closes at 15:00 the day before.
- **Have someone call for you.** For phone-only places, your hotel front desk can often help.
- **Same bath rules apply.** Even in a private bath, wash before you get in and keep your towel out of the water. See our [first-onsen guide](../first-onsen-rules/).
- **Tattoos?** See our [tattoo-friendly onsen guide](../tattoo-friendly-onsen-japan/) for public baths too.
